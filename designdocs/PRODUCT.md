## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: industry recruiters and hiring engineers screening for 2026 internships and 2027 new-grad roles, typically skimming for under a minute from a resume link or LinkedIn. Secondary: faculty, IEEE/industry contacts, and collaborators evaluating research or leadership fit.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Matthew Ketas (BS Computer Engineering, University of Pittsburgh, April 2027). Presents academics, experience, research, projects, and leadership, and makes contact effortless. Success: a recruiter forms a strong impression within seconds, finds concrete evidence fast, and reaches out or downloads the resume.

## Positioning

An undergrad who works across the hardware-software boundary and can prove it: EEG signal pipelines in Python (HearSmart), ESP32 firmware and PCB verification (SaveIt!), C#/.NET desktop application work at Aerotech, and Playwright test infrastructure at Own Company (acquired by Salesforce). The site leads with this breadth; neighboring portfolios cannot truthfully copy it. Do not get too lost in the weeds however - we want to show that projects from both software and hardware have merit - it should not come across as jack of all trades master of none.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence over adjectives — every claim maps to a resume fact.
2. Recruiter's minute — hierarchy serves a 60-second skim; depth available, never required.
3. Hardware-software boundary is the story — structure and visuals reinforce it.
4. Contact is frictionless — email, resume download, and form always one step away.
5. Identity from the subject — PCB/circuit vernacular used where it encodes meaning, not as decoration.
