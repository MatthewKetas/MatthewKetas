Who I am

About.

I’m drawn to the layer where a physical system and the code controlling it have to agree with each other — where a sensor reading has to survive a microcontroller’s GPIO pins, cross a wireless link, and still mean the same thing on the other side. That’s the work I keep choosing: SaveIt’s enclosure and embedded path, HearSmart’s real-time EEG pipeline, a hardware-tester mode at Aerotech. I like systems where being wrong is immediately, physically obvious.

Outside of coursework, I’ve spent two years as Vice President of the University of Pittsburgh IEEE student branch, where I drove event programming strategy that grew attendance more than 100%, coordinated industry partnerships with companies including Hitachi and Aerotech, restructured the chapter’s constitution, and launched its official website.

I’m also Vice President of Recruitment and Wellness for Triangle Fraternity (STEM) at Pitt, after a year as chapter secretary — organizing a full year of recruitment events for incoming engineers and scientists, and coordinating with the executive board on philanthropy, professional, and academic programming.
