How I work

From boundary to behavior.

01 Understand the system

Start with interfaces, constraints, timing, failure modes, and the people who operate the system.

02 Build the path

Choose clear component boundaries, implement the smallest dependable flow, and make state observable.

03 Verify the behavior

Test at the right boundaries, investigate evidence when something fails, and document what was learned.
